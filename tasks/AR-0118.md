## Objective

Turn a service-side SSH alias into a verified, ordered candidate set without
assuming that the alias or split-DNS name is resolvable from Android.

## Acceptance evidence

- `ssh -G` configuration, canonical names, ports, and IPv4/IPv6 candidates are enumerated deterministically.
- Candidate host keys are collected and fingerprinted without logging credentials.
- Invalid, loopback-only, link-local, and ambiguous candidates are reported clearly.
- The public contract exposes candidates and diagnostics without private paths or secrets.
