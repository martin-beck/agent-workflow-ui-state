## Objective

Make registering a phone to a workflow project as easy as starting a project
registration and scanning its QR code with the app camera.

## Acceptance evidence

- End-to-end registration is covered on a local network and through the
  supported remote bootstrap path.
- The user can inspect, cancel, revoke, and replace a registered device.
