## Objective

Connect the phone and workflow service through the selected SSH candidate using
an outbound, authenticated, revision-bound tunnel.

## Acceptance evidence

- Tunnel creation, keepalive, reconnect, teardown, and offline recovery are deterministic.
- Android background restrictions and network changes are handled explicitly.
- Tunnel forwarding is limited to the workflow service endpoint.
- Credential rotation and revocation close existing sessions.
