## Objective

Give the user explicit control when the network, terminal, GUI, or remote host
is unavailable instead of leaving an ambiguous or hanging session.

## Acceptance evidence

- Fault-injected retries are bounded, idempotent, and visibly reported.
- Runtime extraction and temporary files are cleaned on every terminal path.
