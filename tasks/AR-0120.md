## Objective

Make SSH enrollment explicit, revision-bound, least-privilege, revocable, and
safe against QR capture, host impersonation, replay, and unauthorized key
installation.

## Acceptance evidence

- Android generates and retains the private key in Keystore; QR contains no private key.
- Enrollment requires explicit user consent and a one-time expiring token.
- Authorized keys are restricted, attributable, revocable, and removable.
- Candidate host-key fingerprints are verified before authentication.
