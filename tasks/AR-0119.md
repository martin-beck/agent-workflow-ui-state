## Objective

Provide automatic public-address, DNS, VPN, and relay candidates while making
clear that detection is advisory until the phone successfully probes them.

## Acceptance evidence

- Optional remote IP-reflection/STUN/relay discovery is explicit, bounded, and privacy-redacted.
- Candidate ordering prefers stable DNS, VPN, and verified relay endpoints before raw public IPs.
- Android probes candidates and reports per-candidate failure reasons.
- No external discovery dependency is mandatory for local-network operation.
