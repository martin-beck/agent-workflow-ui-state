# AR-0110: Android bridge conformance and live-batch contract

Define the complete Android request/response/event contract for published
decision batches, including canonical Save events, empty/changed batches,
stale revisions, and reconnect behavior. Add schema fixtures and cross-renderer
conformance tests against the TUI and GUI.

Acceptance: every accepted Android event is byte-for-byte equivalent in meaning
to the desktop event; stale, replayed, incomplete, or cross-project messages
are rejected with deterministic errors.
