# AR-0090: Pinned-family compatibility probe

1. Add a machine-readable compatibility probe to the umbrella workflow.
2. Resolve every pinned release and verify the bridge, trigger, and schema
   versions against the checked-in contracts.
3. Run the probe in CI and fail closed on a missing tag, immutable-commit
   mismatch, or incompatible schema.

Acceptance evidence:

- The probe validates the exact UI, Coordinator, Guidance, and Quality pins.
- A tampered, missing, or schema-incompatible pin is rejected in tests.
- The umbrella CI publishes a privacy-safe compatibility report.
