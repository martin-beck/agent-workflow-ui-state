# AR-0105: Android decision bridge and registration contract

1. Define a versioned phone/UI bridge for revision-bound batches, Markdown
   projections, typed decision events, acknowledgements, reconnect, and
   registration identity.
2. Define QR bootstrap payloads, expiry, one-time consumption, revocation,
   privacy boundaries, and Coordinator ownership.

Acceptance evidence:

- Schemas and hostile fixtures reject stale, replayed, cross-project, and
  cross-revision messages.
- Registration and decision events are compatible with the existing TUI/GUI
  envelopes and journal semantics.
