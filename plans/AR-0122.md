# AR-0122: Workflow service SSH bridge and transport fallback

Adapt the project-side service to consume the selected tunnel endpoint while
retaining the current authenticated HTTPS adapter as a fallback. Keep project,
device, session, task revision, packet digest, and sequence checks identical
across transports.

Acceptance includes transport-equivalence fixtures, stale/replay rejection,
and a proof that a failed tunnel cannot silently downgrade authentication.
