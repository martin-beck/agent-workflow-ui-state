# AR-0100: Decision workspace intelligence and batch control

1. Add filtering, grouping, dependency-aware ordering, and progress views for
   large batches spanning multiple ARs/projects.
2. Provide a clear current/remaining/blocked/answered summary with drill-down
   to evidence, affected ARs, conflicts, and proposed implications.
3. Support explicit user-controlled ordering without losing authoritative
   dependency order or batch identity.

Acceptance evidence:

- A large multi-AR fixture can be filtered and completed without dropping
  points or losing anchors.
- Blocked and dependent decisions explain why they cannot proceed.
- GUI and TUI render the same batch status and selection semantics.
