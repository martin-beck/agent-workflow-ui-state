# AR-0106: Android service and reconnect lifecycle

1. Implement the project-side phone service with authenticated registration,
   persistent device identity, heartbeats, reconnect, cancellation, and
   single-active-endpoint routing.
2. Persist only redacted device/session metadata and revision-bound events.

Acceptance evidence:

- Network loss, process restart, duplicate event, revoke, and stale revision
  tests are deterministic and fail closed.
- A registered device receives every newly pending decision and no unregistered
  endpoint receives private packets.
