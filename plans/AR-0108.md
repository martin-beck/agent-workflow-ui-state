# AR-0108: QR registration and phone onboarding

1. Add a project-side registration command that displays a short-lived QR code.
2. Let the Android app scan/import the QR payload, show the project identity
   and permissions, and complete one-time registration with explicit consent.

Acceptance evidence:

- QR payloads never contain reusable credentials and expire/replay-fail.
- Onboarding succeeds without manual host/path/token copying and supports
  cancel, retry, revoke, and replacement-device flows.
