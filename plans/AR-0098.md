# AR-0098: Session recovery, cancellation, and bounded transport control

1. Add explicit reconnect, cancel, timeout, and retry controls to the session
   lifecycle without changing the revision-bound request identity.
2. Bound every SSH, SCP, journal, and renderer subprocess operation and clean
   temporary runtime/request material on success, cancellation, and failure.
3. Make retries idempotent with unique attempt identities and explicit final
   disposition (`completed`, `cancelled`, `timed-out`, or `needs-user`).

Acceptance evidence:

- Interrupted GUI/TUI sessions resume the same batch without duplicate events.
- Missing hosts, hung commands, and partial uploads terminate within a bound.
- Temporary directories and remote staging files are removed or reported.
